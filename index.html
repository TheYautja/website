Home

Welcome. This is my personal website, where I keep track of the things I work on, the things I learn, and whatever else I find worth documenting.

I'm a Brazilian programmer primarily interested in systems programming, Linux and low-level software. I spend most of my time working with C and Dart, experimenting with different technologies and occasionally contributing to open-source projects.

This site also serves as a public record of my work. Feel free to browse the projects, read some of my notes, or get in touch.
